# ADR-039: A proposed decision is not yet asked for a direction

## Context and Problem Statement

Must produce nothing at all. Naming the direction is part of accepting, not of proposing —
that is what the invariant means by «not accepted until reachable from the plan».

## Decision

Stay quiet.
